# diptych

```
spinning in the kitchen
racing thoughts become a blur
my heart is pounding
i can hear you laughing
laughing both of us
and for a moment
, just one moment,
everything’s alright

heel turn
pirouette
arms outstretched and in for speed and 
out again for feeling

join me in a chorus of sweet nothings
incantations spoken with a grin
 joy
stimulations 
wash away my sins my 
 worry 
worry blurry fears my tears 
humble adulations 
 danger
get set go, congratulations
this train has no brakes for when its gone too far

and so we spin

is it enough?

how could it be enough you’re selfless

clothe the poor and feed the sick
heal the hungry laying bricks
tear down my walls and build foundations
shrines cathedrals neuromutations

somewhere for me to sit when done 
when all is set and done i lose 
myself in grand delusions can I truly see you
am I blind or tinted rouge?

of course im blind

my thoughts are racing 

hearts become a blur

so teach me how to spin,
so I can learn to laugh again

rigidity
inflexible thinking
my behavior is 
 stereotyped 
  compulsive 
    injurious

i didn’t know how to cope until I spun with you

its hard to break these patterns
 patterns
patterns a.b. schemes and loops
and cycles laugh cry spin and swoop
our masks split open
ear to ear
be still my dear and disappear

  your cracks are showing 

fear you’ve sown begins to rear 
its ugly head, the gale is blowing

lose your smile in the knowing

choke on blood and feel the rise
of toxic traits i wish weren’t mine

call me a good girl 
, break my face
       my heart 
      the heat 
    the steam 
  embrace 
and sigh in darkness 
pull me in

my pen is breaking through my skin

oh god im falling in again i need a hand i need a friend not 
memories draped in psychosis
schizoaffective lose my focus burn my ends
ozzy auzzie oy! and then


i miss you

   and im sorry
```

```
beneath the sable cover of the night
 the heavens are dark the earth is shining bright
we dance until the fiery light of day
 our masks expose and visage melt away

```
